I'm Jon Currie, the creator of BeeEmUu — a free, open-source BMW diagnostics desktop app that just hit v2.0.0.

Most BMW diagnostic tools are proprietary, subscription-based, or phone apps with ads. BeeEmUu is none of those things. It's GPL-3.0, has no telemetry, and is built with Tauri 2 + Rust.

**What it does:**
- Read/clear fault codes, live data streaming, session logging and replay
- K+DCAN and ENET/DoIP support (most OSS tools stop at K-line; this covers modern Ethernet diagnostics too)
- Built-in CAN simulator for testing without hardware
- 15 BMW-specific CAN decoders (not generic OBD-II)
- Accessibility + i18n (DE/EN/FR)
