# Plan — v4.3.0 "Second Opinion"

**Parent:** `docs/v3.8_to_v5.0_roadmap.md` (schedule of record)
**Tier:** A — pure analysis over community data, plus a read-only panel.
**Depends on:** nothing hard; benefits from v4.2.0 findings being the things
people opine about.

## Why

The community registry already collects repair reports. Today the app shows
them as a list, which means the reader does the statistics in their head — and
a list with two confident wrong answers next to one right one reads the same as
a consensus. This cycle does the arithmetic the reader cannot do reliably:
weight each opinion, surface where the community **agrees**, and — more
usefully — surface where it is **split**, because a split is where the app
should stop and say so.

## Slices

### S1 — Opinion normalisation
`normaliseOpinion(o)` → `{ id, author, verdict, confidence, date, evidence }`.
Missing or unparseable fields become explicit `null`s with the problem named —
they are not filled in from a default.

### S2 — `weightOpinion(opinion, ctx)`
Weight from the inputs the caller supplies, each contributing a bounded factor:
- **corroboration** — does the opinion's evidence cite a channel/finding the
  log actually contains?
- **recency** — decays with the age supplied via injected `now`.
- **author history** — passed in as `ctx.authorReputation`; if the caller has no
  history for an author, that factor is neutral, **not** a penalty. An unknown
  author is unknown, not untrusted.

### S3 — `consensus(opinions, ctx)`
`{ groups, leading, margin, verdict: 'agree' | 'split' | 'thin' | 'none',
confidence, contention, evidence }`.
- `agree` — one group's weight exceeds `AGREE_SHARE` of the total.
- `split` — two or more groups each exceed `CONTENTION_SHARE`; `contention`
  lists them.
- `thin` — too few opinions total (`MIN_OPINIONS`) to call anything.
- `none` — no opinions at all.
`thin` and `none` are distinct, and both are distinct from `agree`.

### S4 — `attribute(opinions, ctx)`
Who said what: `{ by_author, corroborated, uncorroborated }`. Used by the panel
so a claim can always be traced to a person and an evidence citation.

### S5 — Panel — "Second opinion"
The leading verdict, the margin, and — when split — both sides with their
support. The panel shows `thin`/`none` plainly and never renders a split as a
recommendation.

### S6 — Release cut (stamp → gen → test → push → tag `v4.3.0` → publish → deploy).

## Refusal contract

- Fewer than `MIN_OPINIONS` opinions ⇒ `verdict: 'thin'` with `confidence`
  reflecting the shortfall. Three opinions is not a consensus.
- No opinions ⇒ `none`.
- An opinion with no parseable verdict is counted as **uncounted**, and the
  engine reports how many it discarded — silent dropping is how a biased
  sample looks like a clean one.
- `now` is injected; no clock is read.

## Acceptance criteria

- `node --test src/js/test/opinion_consensus.test.cjs` green; full suite green.
- A unanimous set of recent, corroborated opinions is `agree` with a high
  margin.
- A 50/50 set is `split` and lists both sides.
- Two opinions are `thin`, not `agree`.
- An unknown author is weighted neutrally, proven by a test comparing an
  unknown-author opinion against one with a neutral reputation.
- Discarded opinions are reported, not silently ignored.
- Every threshold is an exported constant.

## Non-goals

- Ranking contributors publicly or any reputation scoring invented by the app.
  Reputation is an input the caller supplies and the panel attributes.
- Fetching opinions from the network — it consumes the list it is handed.
