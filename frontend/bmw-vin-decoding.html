BMW VIN decoding.

The 17-character VIN, what each position encodes, and what it does not tell you.

GuideUpdated October 2026GPL-3.0
On this pageThe structureWhat it does not sayRead the modules

The structure

A VIN is 17 characters. The first three are the World Manufacturer Identifier; BMW's WBA/WBS/WBX prefixes identify a BMW passenger car. Position 10 is the model year, position 11 the plant.

What it does not say

The VIN identifies the chassis and build, not the fitted options or the current fault state. Options and software level come from the modules themselves — read them over K+DCAN or ENET.
