Fault-code questions

Why do BMW codes look different from OBD-II codes?

BMW uses 4-hex and 6-hex manufacturer codes alongside the generic SAE J2012 P/B/C/U codes. Both describe the same underlying faults, but the hex codes carry BMW-specific detail the generic set flattens.

Where do the descriptions come from?

Open community research (BimmerFest, Bimmerforums, SpoolStreet, BimmerProfs and others) plus the public-domain SAE definitions. No proprietary ISTA text is reproduced.
